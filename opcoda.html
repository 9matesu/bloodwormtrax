Sound source

An experimental plugin inspired by Ryoji Ikeda, databending and EPROM.

Four views

Control

Engine

  • Eight grain voices with size, density, position, spray, pitch and pan.
  • Grains from 1 ms to 2 s and density from 1 to 200 grains per second.
  • Four grain windows: Hann, Gauss, Hamming and Blackman.
  • Scan moves through the file.
  • Stereo reading with pan and width per grain.

Control

  • ADSR envelope with adjustable curves on all four segments.
  • Two biquad filters in series, with cutoff, resonance, slope and drive.
  • LFO and modulation matrix with eight routes and eight sources.
  • Virtual keyboard and MIDI control in the standalone.
  • Automation and CC in the host, with 66 addressable parameters.

File

  • Four views: wave, bytes, entropy, matrix.
  • Bytes in hexadecimal with the decoded instruction.
  • Entropy is an image of the file, for picking a region to hear.
  • Ten factory presets. .opcoda files save the state.
  • Loads .exe, .dll, .bin and .sys with a valid PE header.
